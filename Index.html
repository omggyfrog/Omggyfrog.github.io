You start with 1,000 play gold and a room at the Rusty Spur Motel. Your goal is the 10,000,000-gold house on Prospect Hill, and the bank wants you to work your way up to a good job first. Getting it is one of the game’s endings, and so is kicking the can. Every ending you find is kept on the Prestige tab. The clock runs on its own, one game minute every 1.5 seconds, and the pause button stops it.

Watch your three bars. Hunger drops as time passes, so order food on your phone: junk food is 15 gold, good food costs more. Health drops from junk food, starving and low sanity, and at zero you land in the hospital. Sanity falls when you lose and comes back from wins, work, food, scrolling and therapy. Pay for your room when you sleep, and after 72 hours awake the stations close. Lose too much, too often, and you’ll kick the can.
